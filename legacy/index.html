The Meaning

Water named after immortality itself.

In the ancient texts, Amruth (अमृत) is the divine nectar of the gods — the elixir of eternal life churned from the cosmic ocean. We carried that spirit into every drop.

Drawn from protected Himalayan springs and balanced with a profile of natural minerals, Amruth Water honours a wisdom thousands of years old: that the purest water is also the most alive.

"Apo hi shtha mayobhuvah" — Water, you are the source of all wellbeing.
