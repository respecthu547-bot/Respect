About Amruth

Nature's Purest Gift, Bottled for You

At Amruth, purity is not a promise — it's our process. The name Amruth means "nectar of life," and that's exactly what every bottle delivers. Our water travels through layers of natural rock for years before reaching our protected springs, picking up essential minerals along the way.

We combine this gift of nature with state-of-the-art multi-stage purification to ensure that what reaches you is as fresh and clean as the source itself.

  • Rich in natural calcium & magnesium
  • Zero artificial additives
  • Ozonised & UV treated
  • Quality certified & ISI approved
